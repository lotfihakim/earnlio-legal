Earnlio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

Data we collect

Email address, a random device identifier generated by the app, your points balance and transaction history, and withdrawal requests (method and amount).

How we use it

To run your account, credit points confirmed by offer partners, prevent duplicate accounts and fraud, and process withdrawals.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Offer partners receive only an anonymous user ID needed to credit your rewards. Our backend is hosted by Supabase.

Retention and deletion

We keep data while your account is active. To delete your account and data, contact us at the email below.

Children

Earnlio is not intended for anyone under 18.
