Earnlio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Earnlio ("we", "us") is an app that lets you earn reward points by completing offers from third-party partners. This policy explains what data we collect and why.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address and password (passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider, we cannot read them). If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email and basic profile information.
  • Activity data: your points balance, transaction history, offers you complete, and withdrawal requests (including the payout details you enter).
  • Device data: a device identifier (Android ID) and basic technical information, used only to detect fraud and abuse such as multiple accounts. We store only a one-way cryptographic hash of the identifier (not the identifier itself), for example to limit the daily bonus to one claim per device per day.

2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To credit points for completed offers and process withdrawals.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and duplicate accounts.
  • To contact you about your account or support requests.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

3. Google Sign-In

If you choose "Continue with Google", we use Google only to verify your identity and get your email. We do not access your contacts, files, or other Google data.

4. Third parties

  • Supabase hosts our database and authentication.
  • Offer partners / offerwall networks receive an anonymous user ID so they can report completed offers to us. They have their own privacy policies.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this page.

We may also disclose data if required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app (Account, then Delete account), which removes your account and associated data. You can also email us to request deletion. Some records may be kept briefly where needed to prevent fraud or meet legal obligations.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and protected by access rules on our database. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. Children

Earnlio is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data by emailing us.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above.
